# Problem frame — ChaseFlow Invoices

## Raw idea
SaaS for agency operators and freelancers: automated invoice chasing with polite multi-channel follow-ups, payment links, and cash-flow forecast from outstanding receivables

## Problem statement
Users waste time and attention chasing unpaid invoices with inconsistent follow-ups. Late payments hurt cash flow and the process is manual and awkward.

## Who hurts most
- Freelancers and small agencies who send invoices regularly
- Operators who already use partial workarounds (templates, spreadsheets)

## Why now
Multi-channel messaging APIs and payment links make automated, polite chasing practical.

## MVP job-to-be-done
Send a polite chase sequence and see expected cash-flow impact in under 5 minutes.
